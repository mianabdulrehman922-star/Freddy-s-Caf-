Our Heritage & Passion

Preserving Pakistan’s Rich Culinary Tapestry

Situated on Umar Saeed Road in Gulberg III, Lahore, AlKhan Heritage brings together the timeless grandeur of Mughal-inspired architecture and the rich, slow-cooked flavours of regional Pakistani gastronomy.

From our sizzling Shinwari BBQ coals to rich handi karahis and fragrant biryanis, every dish is crafted with authentic ground spices, fresh hand-picked produce, and centuries-old culinary tradition. Whether seated in our grand indoor halls or amidst our illuminated garden fountains, experience hospitality crafted for life’s grand celebrations.
